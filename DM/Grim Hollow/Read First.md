This campaign setting explores a dark world embodying five main themes.
1. Broken World
	* Good will only for a price.
	* Monsters prey the wild, sins prey the city.
	* The good ol' days are gone.
2. Dead Gods
	* Prayers go unanswered
	* Few wield divine magic
	* Secular powers view such magic as a threat
3. Magic is Mistrusted
	- Governments regulate or even lead inquisitions.
	- Citizens shun or even attack any magic users
4. Humanity is Flawed
	- Just one bad day.
	- Les Misérables.
	- Suspect everyone's motives.
5. Hope Within the Darkness
	- There are those still willing to make the ultimate sacrifice for others.
	- Even the small acts matter
	- Celebrate the victories, no matter how small
