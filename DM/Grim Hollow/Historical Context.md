# Era of Antiquity
## Creation Stories
### The Gods' Awakening and The Aetheric War
The [[DM/Grim Hollow/Nations/Bürach Empire/Summary|Büracher]] believe the world was created with the awakening of the gods.

But then the Aether Kindred emerged from the void and ruptured the heavens and destroyed the gods leaving only the [[DM/Grim Hollow/Pantheons/Four Divines|Four Divines]]. The Celestials and [[DM/Grim Hollow/Pantheons/Arch Seraphs|Arch Seraphs]] who served the gods that died, fell from grace and dwell in the Netherworld.

### The Primordic Cycle
The [[DM/Grim Hollow/Nations/Valikan Clans/Summary|Valikan]] claim that the world consisted of only the primordials and their ruler Gormadraug, the Great Wyrm. The mortal realm itself was formed from the corpse of the Great Wyrm when Kentigern vanquished him.

### Hymn of Viduami
In [[DM/Grim Hollow/Nations/Ostoyan Empire/Summary|Ostoya]], people talk about a death god, older than creation, who was entombed by the others gods. When early mortals discovered the tomb of this god, they imparted their divine essence into the mortals, creating immortal yet dead creatures, the first [[Vampires]].

## Early Mortals
### Twilight of the Dreamers
Possibly the first peoples of the mortal realm. Their society was lost to an unknown cataclysm. Though a group of mystics known as the dreamers survived to modern days by entering a deep slumber.

### Birth of Caer Neiada
Archfey (or fallen seraphs depending on who you ask) cultivated Caer Neiada and the ulufey settle the land.

### Fall of Ember Cairn
The ancestral home of the [[DM/Grim Hollow/Species/Dragonborn|dragonborn]] is destroyed. Survivors scatter across Etharis as nomads.

### Kingdoms of Stone
The dwarves are the first to build fortresses and kingdoms in the very heart of Etharis.

### Exodus of the Orcs
The orcs that once dwelled in northern Etharis, sailed west to distant lands, seeking not only battles but something more.

### Human Tribes
Humanity is nothing more than small tribes dwelling in the western forests.

# Era of Expansion
Human tribes gradually spread over the expanse of Etharis, subjugating or pushing out the neighboring species. Many small kingdoms rise and fall.

## Human Kingdoms
### Four Kingdoms of the Divines
The largest human kingdoms take hold of central Etharis. They are the [[DM/Grim Hollow/Nations/Bürach Empire/Provinces/Nordenland|Norden]], [[DM/Grim Hollow/Nations/Bürach Empire/Provinces/Rauland|Raufolk]], [[DM/Grim Hollow/Nations/Bürach Empire/Provinces/Unterland|Unters]], and [[DM/Grim Hollow/Nations/Bürach Empire/Provinces/Abendland|Abendlanders]].

### Arrival of the First Men
Settlers traveling south navigate through the Grove Maze and eventually discover Caer Neiada.

### Wars of Stehlenwald and Grabenstein
The warlike [[DM/Grim Hollow/Nations/Bürach Empire/Provinces/Unterland|Unters]] claim the dwarven city Grabenstein while Stehlenwald endures the siege.

### The Bürach Empire
Emperor Indorius I is ordained by the Hearthkeepers as the chosen of the [[DM/Grim Hollow/Pantheons/Four Divines|Four Divines]].

### Crossing the Grey Spine
Emigrants from the empire travel east discovering remains of an ancient civilization and begin to settle there, forming what would later become Ostoya.

### Castinellan Peninsula
The empire reaches to the southeast corner of Etharis and discovers the remains of a [[DM/Grim Hollow/Species/Dragonborn|dragonborn]] kingdom

### Founding of Morencia
Refuges attempting to escape the subjugation of the Bürach Empires take to a hidden island in the southern sea, establishing the haven of Morencia.

### Pact of the Sacred Land
An agreement between the humans and the elves to create the united kingdom of [[DM/Grim Hollow/Nations/Charneault Kingdom/Summary|Charneault]].

The king and nobility would govern the land, and accept counsel from the Circle of Bondmakers about the whims of the archfey.

Chapter knights are established to protect the whole land and the sacred sites of the elves would not be disturbed by human expansion.

The elven cities would be surrendered to Human powers, except for Tol Leyemil. But in exchange, the king must send away their first-born to join the Fey Courts.

## Valikan Sagas

### Thorgard
This hero unites the Valikan clans and directs their conquest southward. Thorgard himself chooses to sail west in search of his orcish kin.

### Cold Iron Keep
Raised by an evil mage, Mordu, who was shortly slain by the Valikan.

### Prismatic Circle
archdruid Kybard has visions that Gormadraug is still alive, and that one day he may awaken. To appease him, blood must be continuously shed through conflict. 

### The *Lord Edda*
Historians of Valika compile the oral traditions, myths, and sagas of their people.

### Flickers of Coldfire
Valika in the Northeast spot a magical flame that leaves a trail of frozen tundra, forming an ice bridge.

## Writings of Numena
Raakan el-Adakar's research on the nature of arcane magic, believing it to originate from a plane of pure energy called Numena and capable of altering the physical plane.

# Era of Descent
The history of the Bürach Empire is defined by defeats and gradual erosion over the course of 200 years.
## Factions Form
With geographical borders settled, ideological ones develop across Etharis

### Company of Free Swords
Darion Harkin offers his services to the Bürach Empire to stamp out the Falaris resistance, as a reward the Empire provides him the resources to found the Company of Free Swords, an international mercenary guild reputed for their honor codes.

### Augustine Trading Company
Manoch Augustus leaves the Castinellan clergy to build a trading company. The company, preaching free trade and education, engages in any and all exchanges to satisfy it's avarice.

### Ebon Syndicate
The famous bandit Hiram Greyman upon losing his crew and nearly his life in a robbery gone wrong, decides to establish a spy network that eventually has ears in every major city of Etharis.

## The First Bürach-Ostoyan War 
A war of secession led by Saint Dayla, Lady of Vengence. Ostoya secured independence with a victory at Fallowheart.

## Unification
The Castinellan Provinces are unified by the Arcanist Inquisition. The nation becomes hostile to all non-divine magic users under a flag of obedience to the church. Only members of the Occultist Guild are permitted to practice arcane magic but this permission may soon be revoked. Nature itself is vilified as primal magic becomes associated with arcane magic.

With the wealth of the Castinellan Provinces amassed, most of these resources are spent on Church-sponsored festivals for the people.

### Reconstruction
The remains of the ancient city of Ember Cairn were chosen to be the foundation of the Arcanist Inquisition stronghold. Any magical history of the city is now hidden beneath the ongoing reconstruction.

## The Red Mourning.
The Bürach Empire employs the service of Analita von Raiza to claim the free city of Liesech. However, the Vampire desired the city for herself and, after uncovering certain the secrets of the town, kills the reigning family. This begins a war with an unseen force called the Filth Grazer and ends with the spread of the Weeping Pox infection.

## Crowning of Emperor Leopold I
Leopold I was a cruel manipulative man who through the divine artifacts tainted his world and even the gods with eldritch corruption. As divisions and rivalries developed, a small group formed amongst the provinces of Bürach to assassinate the emperor and save the nation. With the emperor dead his influence on the gods faded, but it was too late. 

# Era of the Beast
With the connection to the gods and ethereal  planes severed, Etharis is now in a state of corruption. A shadow now prowls the land, turning the minds of many into wickedness and some into aberrant horrors. The people of Etharis simply refer to this shadow as the "Great Beast" and it's path can be spotted by the sigil of a deer skull crowned with many antlers.

## The God's End
The battle of the gods was already in motion to destroy the world, yet Aurelia, the first to regain her senses, shielded the Material Plane at the moment of utter calamity. Now the gods are gone and the world of Etharis is populated by new creatures who have lost their plane of existence to the calamity.

### Aftermath
The divine artifacts are lost, though no one truly knows why. Stories are told that the assassins quarreled over whose province should possess the artifacts until it was decided that no individual should ever be allowed to wield all four again. Others claim that with the death of the Gods, the Artifacts simply vanished. But they believe one day, a hero will return all four artifacts to Bürach and restore the world to as it was under the first emperor.

## The Second Bürach-Ostoyan War 
With a need for unity under the new emperor of Bürach, a second war was declared on Ostoya.

### The Darkfall
The sun vanishes from the Ostoyan Empire, and the country's hero returns with a Sanguine curse. The sovereignty of Ostoya is assured, but people continue to pay the price in blood to their new rulers the Crimson Court.

## The Dark Mist
As the firstborn daughter of the king of Charneault was being transported to the Temple of Mist, her convoy was attacked. Her attackers continued to the Temple and they were pursued by the Princess' former lover.

It is unknown what happened in the end, except that the Pact of the Sacred Land was broken as a dark mist filled with terrors began to cover the kingdom.

## Fractions Form
New groups form in response to the crisis.

### Watchers of the Faithful
With the gods dead, the Castinellan theocracy attempts to preserve the faith by calling for devotion to the Arch Seraphs who remain. Seeking to win over hearts and minds through words and writings.

### Order of Kentigern
The coldfire crisis has turned into an eruption. This order is the only thing keeping the rest of Etharis from turning into a frozen tundra.

### The Raevo Rebellion
The rebellion ends with Ostoya split in two and Malkovia balancing independence from either faction.
