**Presentation**: As other Heatherkeepers, bears and emblem of a hawk on his chest and wears a black chasuble with pointed shoulders

**Vocalization**: quiet, contemplative, but gives fiery sermons.

**Occupation**: Bishop, preacher

**Location**: Abendland

**Motivation**: Preaches on the Great Beast as a second omen from the Gods, against the teachings of his fellow Hearthkeeprs

**Connection**: The emperor is fascinated with him, but his opponents claim he's a member of the Celestial Gate
