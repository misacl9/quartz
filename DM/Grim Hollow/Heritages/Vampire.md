# Notes
## Vampiric Bloodlines
**Vampiric Bloodlines**: The manifestations of vampirism have diversified over the centuries.
- Soman: Lavish and graceful in appearance.
- Upyr: Corpselike and functional
- Fzeg: Literal mutts, vampire and werewolf.
- Strigoi: Gangly and narrow.
- Ecoukrylo: Batlike in appearance
