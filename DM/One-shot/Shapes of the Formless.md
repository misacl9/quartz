- **Setting**: Wadlands, searching for an ancient temple
- **Context:** A human merchant (secretly cultist) named Melchis wishes to find the temple for an Artifact, the tesseract.
- **Motivation:** Melchis has offered 2,000 GP in trade bars to those who can take him there and back safely.
- **Player engagement**: Fighting off monsters that may attack the party and exploring the temple
- **Notes:** Rolling 16 in Welcome to the Jungle is equivalent to fighting the Final boss

### The Ruins (High)
#### Approaching the temple
- 9 days, 8 of heavy rain
	- Lightly Obscured conditions
	- Risk of quicksand pit
		- sink 1d4 ft (10 max)
		- Restrained condition
		- Athletics check DC10 + ft
		- Help, Athletics check DC5 + ft
		- Two Giant Scorpions start attacking

#### Within the temple
- Refer to Dungeon Hideout map in app. B
	- roll for 4 entrences
	- Artifact located in Northwest room
- Encounter with **XP: 3,300**
	- 5 Nothics spread around ruins
- Melchis rushes toward artifact when not in combat
	- If he puts it on turns into a Red Slaad
	- If PC puts it on, make a DC 15 Charisma check or get possessed
- IF Defeat Melchis
	- Searching reveals Bag of Holding, 250 GP of trade bars, Spell Scroll of Teleportation Circle
- Destroying the Artifact
	- Upon casting Identify
	- Only possible by traveling to Mechanus in the Outer Planes
