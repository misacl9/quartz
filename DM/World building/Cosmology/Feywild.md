## Greater Domains of Delight
### Fablerise
- mirrors black woods of material plane
- Archfey enjoys story telling
- Up to CR 5 encounters (excluding Betsy)
### Gloaming court 
- mirrors safari state of material plane
- Archfey enjoy being merry and gay without regulation
- Up to CR 6 encounters
### Prismeer
- mirrors Wadlands of material plane
- A forrest, swamp, and mountain
- Archfey grants wishes, archhags make deals
- Up to CR 21 encounters
### Summer court
- mirrors Austral of material plane
- Noble
- Up to CR 7 encounters
