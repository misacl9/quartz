### Recap
Traveling southeast, to accomplish something, our adventures saw a group of jackals along the main road. Bowlda recognized some significance of the jackals and spoke with one to know what it wanted. It asked for a freshly killed wild boar which the ranger of the party was able to locate and kill. With the jackals appeased, our adventurers approached Lumbasa, the capital of the safari states. Dartfrogdash knew a good amount of the neighborhoods of the capital and the group agreed to go to Scute Side for it's proximity to The Circle in hopes of finding a senator at a bar. They found a tavern and chatted with the barkeep to confirm that their is an ongoing civil unrest. Within the same tavern, they approached a Leonin politician with his friends and presented themselves as entertainers in hopes of being invited to some party. Our adventurers were successful and got invited to the senator's daughter's sweet sixteen at 9 pm on Tallusday the 23rd. They wisely stopped by the national library to read up on Leonin culture to know how to plan for the party (loud booming sounds, traditional coming of age roar). Afterwards, they decided to pass the time as vigilantes and crack down on drug trafficking in the Watersheds. They tailed a drunken Vanara to a shady bar where they decided to get some information. Bowlda and Dartfrogdash wasted time flirting with the bar keep Cleo and the waitress La-a, who were both irritated by the encounter. Rushka got to the point and asked some brutish tortles for some drugs and managed to make an arrangement to meet with their supplier for some spice crystals. Now our adventurers need only wait for the tortles to finish their arm wrestling match which had already lasted a half hour.


### Summary
After patiently waiting, the tortles take our characters to a nearby dingy outpost. Within the outpost awaits Nazar one of the local suppliers of dragons fang. Sadly negotiations broke down and the party was asked to leave, though Rushka chose to be stubborn and caused a fight to break out. The party was able to wipe out the outpost and had decided to burn the whole place down. Luckily, Austyn in his wisdom wanted to search for any items that could be STORY RELEVEANT and help with CONTINUING THE PLOT. They discovered a document indicating a drop of dragon's fang near the ivory towers scheduled for the same date as the Leonin senators party.
