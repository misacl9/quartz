### Recap
The group decides to attend the Senator's party to improve their reputation and collect info on current political dealings. They have an easy time entering the Ivory Towers and reaching the senator's suite. They meet with the head butler Bartholomeow who directs them to setup by the balcony pool. As they setup to perform they're able to easily identify the birthday girl. As people are distracted by the performance, Rushka with his sharp insight is able to discern the "social hierarchy" of the teens. Of note, he realizes that the senator's daughter is making eyes to the pool boy, a Vanara by the name of Chaz. The party tries to socialize with Chaz to determine his relation with the Senator's family. After witnessing Chaz's body language, Bowlda is able to pick up that certain servants are behaving differently, as if they are less familiar with the suite layout. These servants are also mostly Tabaxi. The party then get's ready for their main performance in the main living room of the suite, they tried to gleam more information as they walked but were too focused on giving a good performance. Just before they get on, the had a brief exchange with the previous performer who went to the green room. Austyn delivered an emotional ballad of a noble family and a father reconciling with his daughter. This won over the senator, who expressed gratitude at their ability to arrive as his usual performers were unable to make it. He then directed them to chat with Bartholomeow who delivered a special thank you cigar for their performance. Our crew then go to the side of the balcony to enjoy their smoke break, however Rushka suddenly falls unconscious and Austyn becomes heavily drowsy. At the sound of a scream, the party realizes that the daughter is being kidnapped and most of the party has been drugged. The party engages with the kidnappers as they try to run away. Before the kidnappers are fully defeated, one called out "Sumeni" and the previous performer stepped out of the green room and attacked our party. Sumeni was able to kidnap the daughter and drag her to the elevator. Rushka then took a leap of faith and jumped off the Ivory Towers to catch him while the rest of the party tended to the party guests. After flying to the ground, Rushka is apprehended by guards who are surrounding the building.
