### Recap
As Rushka recovers he receives this message in a dream "The deep chill claimed you. Though now free, the frost lingers. Ice binds all to me." After deliberating the group decides they have more reason to travel northward to Alwayswinter. At the base of Mt. Veilcrest they prepare at a Goliath town dedicated to timberwork. Ascending the mountain they fought off Ice Mephit and took a short rest at an outpost where they met a monk. With the Monk's directions they were able to find the sister temple of Br. Meep and confront the ice wyrmling residing within. Br. Meep successfully transformed a potential combat encounter into a lecture on defying authoritarian powers and convinced the pubescent wyrmling into letting the Monk's return (albeit still choosing to be a nuisance). Their monk friend though wasn't eager at the idea of sharing the temple until he noticed Austyn's magical comprehension of languages. He asked our adventurers to go on a quest to Mt. Dreadspire to visit Dirka the mage and ask for scrolls to be provided for the monks to use with the wyrmling. Agreeing to this our adventurers had to first pass Mt. Anders where Rushka experienced another dream calling him up the mountain. The party continued to Mt. Dreadspire and camped at the base at a fishing village. It was there they got the idea to get some trophy to appease Dirka and as such joined captain Halfass on his quest to kill the lake monster ol' Hardass


### Summary
Captain Halfass baited the monster and the adventurers successfully dispatched him, but not without Brother Meep perishing. As the captain took his share of the booty, the adventurers decided how to handle the remains and prep for the trek up Mt. Dreadspire. While in town they met a group of monks checking in on them, who took Br. Beeps body and provided a new monk Br. Meep to join our adventurers. Before climbing the mountains, our adventurers prepared well, turning the monster's shell into a usable sled, bartering with the local dogsled owner for 8 dogs at a cheap rate of 1 gp, and preparing sufficient food for them and the dogs. As a bonus, they prepared a giant wine skin from the intestines and filled it with a local fish fermentation, bass ale, 2 sp.
