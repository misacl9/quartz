### Recap
Captain Halfass baited the monster and the adventurers successfully dispatched him, but not without Brother Meep perishing. As the captain took his share of the booty, the adventurers decided how to handle the remains and prep for the trek up Mt. Dreadspire. While in town they met a group of monks checking in on them, who took Br. Beeps body and provided a new monk Br. Meep to join our adventurers. Before climbing the mountains, our adventurers prepared well, turning the monster's shell into a usable sled, bartering with the local dogsled owner for 8 dogs at a cheap rate of 1 gp, and preparing sufficient food for them and the dogs. As a bonus, they prepared a giant wine skin from the intestines and filled it with a local fish fermentation, bass ale, 2 sp.


### Summary
Our adventurers scaled the wintry mountainside, along the way they reached an area of dead trees and razer sharp icicles where they were ambushed by a yeti. Thanks to Rushka's quick thinking he was able to intimidate the yeti with a whip strike and send it wimpering away, however they had already lost one dog. After a few more hours our adventurers reached the top of the mountain which seemed to be strangely warmer and more vibrant. At the peak they found a cottage where they encountered the great mage Dirka. In exchange for the scroll of comprehend languages, Dirka asked them to bring a yeti for company. The adventurers gave Dirka a shell which got them a scroll to help them with capturing the yeti. The adventureres came up with a plan to lay a trap in front of the Yeti's cave, where they would cast the spell on the yeti or tie it up. The spell failed, but luckily their backup did not. They tied the yeti up like a pinata and proceeded to maul it unconscious until it's fur had been melted off by acid. Upon seeing the condition of the yeti, Dirka gave the adventurers what they wanted and asked them to please leave. Upon returning to town, the adventurers somehow convinced the sleigh dog owner they only bought 6 dogs and returned with a stray and Rushka experienced a weird piss bear dream. To reach the monastery on Mt. Veilcrest, our adventurers had to tough through a blizzard but made it off easy. With their quest completed, Rushka decided to find out more about the cult on Mt. Anders and learned about the Frostmaiden, an archfey looking to build her power. Upon deliberation the group decided not to confront her and instead head southbound to address other issues in the Safari States.
