### Recap
Making the trek down to the safari states our adventurers resorted to stealing and bargaining to get by. Along the way they've heard news of goblin forces displaying farmers in Neverwinter, a cult growing in Alwayswinter, and growing riots in the safari states. They've also noticed disputed claims in the Feywild and Weave destabilizing, though whether this is a natural cycle or man made is debated. As our adventurers reach the border town of Cola, they decide the best way to navigate customs is by knocking out the guard and making a run for it. To acquaint themselves with the town they first walk through the Artisan's Place, where they notice stores covered in feces and forges defaced by banana symbols. Upon questioning one of the Gnomish blacksmiths they realize that Cola is struggling with anti-immigrant sentiments and civil rights protests. They decide to find a shady Caravanserai to rest at where they meet up with Bowlda, a halfling ranger who specializes in baking and decorating. Bowlda requests the party's services to help him kill his love rival. After some bickering between Bowlda and Rushka they were voluntold by the barkeep to just party up and get some rest. The following day, the party heads out to the hanging markets to get items and information. While they weren't able to purchase anything after several poor impressions with the vendors. Rushka identified a thieves vendor who communicated to them about plans for a potential riot happening tonight in the town park. Our adventurers scoped out the scene and identified the bell tower of the temple as a good place to stake out. In the evening a group of Vanara Rights protesters gathered on the side of the park facing The Manes (the elitist neighborhood). Leonin officers surrounded the protesters with the bulk of the force focused on protecting The Manes. For some reason, Rushka gained the inspiration to grab Bowlda and hold him over the tower screaming "Nants ingonyama bagithi Baba". The crowd was mostly confused, but one of the officers recognized Rushka and some guards went up the tower to arrest him. It was during this confusion that someone in the crowd casted Thunderwave at the remaining officers and pandemonium erupted.


### Summary
The battle was gruesome, only 1 banana clan member and 3 guards (including the captain and second in command) survived the skirmish. The adventurers refrained from picking a side (including their own) as they went off with different objectives. Austyn tried to calm the fight, Rushka coated Bowlda in oil, and Beep Borp proceeded to melt the guards chasing after them and smash a banana clan member in his way. Eventually Beep Borp was knocked unconscious and Austyn and Bowlda convinced the guards they were not the enemy and that the real culprit was Rushka who proceeded to fly away and escape (with Bowlda as a hostage). But Bowlda was able to get out just as Rushka flew upwards and "miraculously" bouncing off the ground proceeds to land on Beep Borp causing permanent damage. The Leonin guards then take our remaining adventurers to the jailhouse. The following day after some questioning, the guards decide to release our adventurers, except Beep Borp, who died in the holding cells and was refused medical aid. Austyn and Bowlda then head to the Artisan's Place to recharge at a local inn and restock. While at the inn they find Rushka, who is chatting with a White Dragonborn named Sheep Shorp. Rushka attempts to make amends with Bowlda by buying him a shot of kombucha, in return, Bowlda dumps oil on him. After some deliberations on their goals, the group decides to prioritize vengeance and help Rushka find his parent's assassin and Bowlda find his rival. As one goal requires traveling east and the other south the group decides to split the difference and go southeast because why the frick not.
