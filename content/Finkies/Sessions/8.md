### Recap
The battle was gruesome, only 1 banana clan member and 3 guards (including the captain and second in command) survived the skirmish. The adventurers refrained from picking a side (including their own) as they went off with different objectives. Austyn tried to calm the fight, Rushka coated Bowlda in oil, and Beep Borp proceeded to melt the guards chasing after them and smash a banana clan member in his way. Eventually Beep Borp was knocked unconscious and Austyn and Bowlda convinced the guards they were not the enemy and that the real culprit was Rushka who proceeded to fly away and escape (with Bowlda as a hostage). But Bowlda was able to get out just as Rushka flew upwards and "miraculously" bouncing off the ground proceeds to land on Beep Borp causing permanent damage. The Leonin guards then take our remaining adventurers to the jailhouse. The following day after some questioning, the guards decide to release our adventurers, except Beep Borp, who died in the holding cells and was refused medical aid. Austyn and Bowlda then head to the Artisan's Place to recharge at a local inn and restock. While at the inn they find Rushka, who is chatting with a White Dragonborn named Sheep Shorp. Rushka attempts to make amends with Bowlda by buying him a shot of kombucha, in return, Bowlda dumps oil on him. After some deliberations on their goals, the group decides to prioritize vengeance and help Rushka find his parent's assassin and Bowlda find his rival. As one goal requires traveling east and the other south the group decides to split the difference and go southeast because why the frick not.


### Summary
Traveling southeast, to accomplish something, our adventures saw a group of jackals along the main road. Bowlda recognized some significance of the jackals and spoke with one to know what it wanted. It asked for a freshly killed wild boar which the ranger of the party was able to locate and kill. With the jackals appeased, our adventurers approached Lumbasa, the capital of the safari states. Dartfrogdash knew a good amount of the neighborhoods of the capital and the group agreed to go to Scute Side for it's proximity to The Circle in hopes of finding a senator at a bar. They found a tavern and chatted with the barkeep to confirm that their is an ongoing civil unrest. Within the same tavern, they approached a Leonin politician with his friends and presented themselves as entertainers in hopes of being invited to some party. Our adventurers were successful and got invited to the senator's daughter's sweet sixteen at 9 pm on Tallusday the 23rd. They wisely stopped by the national library to read up on Leonin culture to know how to plan for the party (loud booming sounds, traditional coming of age roar). Afterwards, they decided to pass the time as vigilantes and crack down on drug trafficking in the Watersheds. They tailed a drunken Vanara to a shady bar where they decided to get some information. Bowlda and Dartfrogdash wasted time flirting with the bar keep Cleo and the waitress La-a, who were both irritated by the encounter. Rushka got to the point and asked some brutish tortels for some drugs and managed to make an arrangement to meet with their supplier for some spice crystals. Now our adventurers need only wait for the tortles to finish their arm wrestling match which had already lasted a half hour.
