### Description
Gnome Barbarian, young adult (69), stoic

Trinket: scrap of cloth from your platoons guidon.

Origin: Not as creative as other Gnomes, so was put to work to testing the inventions. Later displayed an aptitude for weaponry that helped him climb the rankings of the Gnomish military.  


### Rank
Sergeant

### Personal Quest
- Capture the Banana Clan.

### Backstory
- Served as a Sergeant during the War. But lost his squad to an ambush from the Banana Clan. This earned a dishonorable discharge and a branding on the face/hands.
