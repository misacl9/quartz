Will you fight the monster within or embrace it?

**Aberrant Horror**: Break free from your physical restrictions at the cost of losing your sense of "self".

**Fey**: Practice faerie magic but be bound to the seasons of the fey court.

**Hag**: Learn skills beyond the fey court, trading purity for power.

**Lich**: Sacrifice family and friends for immortality and world-rending powers.

**Lycanthrope**: The body of a beast, and the mind of one...

**Ooze**: Watch the fat, organs, bones, skin, and thoughts melt away!

**Primordial**: Master the elements, but slip up, and get shredded by the primordial chaos.

**Seraph**: Become a beacon of light, attracting friends and foes alike.

**Shadowsteel Ghoul**: Take performance-enhancing drugs for your magic. It won't harm your organs but it'll leave an indelible mark on your soul.

**Specter**: Forfeit an afterlife and cling to the material world, only complete oblivion awaits should you die again.

**Vampire**: Rule the night, but never know peace as you try to sate your appetite.
