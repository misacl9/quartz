### Recap
Gastro and Hydrosis find themselves in the sterile white lands of mordent, where the only discernable color on the landscapes is gray smog from gray towns. As our adventurers try to make sense of the world they're in, the locals direct them to the greatest scientific mind of the lands, Dr. Viktra Mordenhiem. Upon meeting the doctor, the party is promised a place to stay and access to her lab equipment IF they help hunt down her missing creation the Unbroken Heart. They agree and sail to the last known location of the Heart at the Isles of Agony. Their they encounter a group of whalers who were stranded on the island after the fleshless whale Rehab destroyed their ship. The whalers tell them how a mishappen monster abducted their captain, Doby Mick, and ask the adventurers to rescue him. They agree and set off in the direction they pointed which seems to align with a mysterious glow at the center of the island. The whalers warn them to be on the look out for the Children, unholy constructs that are an abomination to the god Talamare. Walking through the snowy forests, the adventurers spot a cluster of motionless humanoid figures. Upon further inspection they discovered the figures to be constructs of flesh and metal, and completely unresponsive to their presence. They decided best to leave the constructs alone. Walking further towards a hill up ahead, the adventurers stopped in their tracks when the hill rose up and looked around revealing the head of an owlbear. They immediately stepped into action to take down the beast. Just as they were about to reach the heart of the island, one of the trees stepped forward and revealed itself to be a troll. The adventurers struggled to kill the beast, every time it fell it came back up, every time it was cut apart, the remains would wriggle back to life. Things were looking desperate until the party realized they could use fire to stop the trolls regeneration. As they continued to fight to the very last, the air around them began to billow as snow and smoke clouded their sight and the mists took them once more.
