### Recap
Six people walked into the mist, two "others" walked out. Gastro and Fitzwilliam found themselves in the lands of mordent, a dreary, gray, decrepit place. Where the people are superstitious and hauntings are frequent. As the two walk past a shop, they encounter a worried old-woman pacing back and forth. Talking with the lady, Beatrice, they learn that the herbalist shop has recently become haunted. She offer's 400 gp to anyone who can exorcise the spirit. The two, doubting the reality of spirits, accept the quest believing to make easy money. Beatrice also tells them that her friend Gennifer is still in the shop. As they enter the building, they spot nothing of the ordinary and decide to split up. As Gastro made his way to the second floor, he felt an overbearing presence attempting to push him down. But Gastro proved the stronger and marched himself upstairs. As he was turning back to warn Fitzwilliam that invisible forces do seem to be at play, he locks eyes with a cat that manifested on the stairwell's banister. At that very moment it pounces through  him, turning into a spectral panther. As the two engage, the panther deals significant damage to Gastro before disappearing through the walls. Gastro and Fitzwilliam reconvene and decide to try and get Gennifer out as fast as possible. Entering the guest bedroom, they spot Gennifer standing motionlessly in the room staring blankly at a wall, as if she's possessed. As they attempt to move her out the spectral panther engages in combat again. After several turns, the party succeeds in damaging the wraith and escaping the building. They rush out to see the old lady behaving erratically moving items into a sack, out of suspicion, Gastro brutally cuts the old lady down. As her shrieks fill the night, Gastro awkwardly heals her wounds and the party proceed to bring Gennifer to a priest to end the possession. After recuperating at the church, Gennifer tells the party how to exercise the spirit. Moving swiftly, the party locate the "mysteriously" broken cat statue in the shop and bring it down to the cellar to perform the ritual. The panther intervenes for one last time, as they exchange blows, Fitzwilliam ends the wraith and its possession on the shop.

### Summary
Gastro and Hydros walk out of the mists into the dense forests of Bürach. The two get a sense of their surroundings by visiting a local tavern bar. Whilst there, they overhear a shaggy old man bemoaning himself to the other patrons who try to ignore him. As the man walks up to the adventurers he presents them a magical eye that gives the wielder luck but seems to torment him with nightmarish visions. He explains how a mysterious man with writing on his face handed it to him, and how no matter how hard he tries to get rid of the eye, he struggles to let go of it and seemingly always finds it back in his pocket. Now he seeks adventurers to take him to the next town over where the man traveled to. He offered all he had, 2 GP, and our adventurers accepted. Traveling through the dense forests our adventurers faced off against a tedious giant spider swarm and a monster fish capable of swinging through the trees with black tendrils. They ran into an escaping Arcanist who they promptly returned to the Inquisitor Knight when he explained his crime for being an Arcanist. They also witnessed a local lynching as a young woman is burned for being a lycanthrope, and proceed to mingle with the villagers to ascertain if the mysterious man passed through. Finally our adventurers arrive to the nearby town of Kumar, where they spot the man preaching to the locals. They interrupted him mid-speech and demand he take back the eye. The herald promised that the eye would soon liberate the man from his material torments and body, but the adventurers were not having any of it. Offended by their rejection, he took back the eye so that it might be given to someone more deserving.
